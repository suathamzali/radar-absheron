      NASA Space Apps 2026 · Dancing with the SARs

      Can a satellite watch Azerbaijan's land change, through clouds and in the dark?

      NISAR is a satellite that sends radio waves to Earth and listens to the echo, a bit like a bat. This site shows, in plain words, what those echoes tell us about our region.

      1. Learn to read radar colours in one minute.
      2. Watch a season of NISAR passes over the Kura fields, Baku and the Caspian, and the day the land felt the rain.
      3. Click any place to read its own radar history.
